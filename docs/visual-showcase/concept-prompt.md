# AFTERFORM visual concept storyboard

```text
Original art direction storyboard for an immersive visual-only website named AFTERFORM. A wide landscape board divided into four equal edge-to-edge cinematic panels with narrow dark gutters. No text, no logos, no UI, no labels. The exact same recognizable asymmetric folded ribbon sculpture evolves across all four panels. Panel 1: a single polished silver chrome folded ribbon floating toward the right in an immense graphite-black gallery, directional studio lighting, large negative space on left. Panel 2: extreme macro of the same ribbon becoming liquid mercury with fine physical ripples and subtle vermilion reflections. Panel 3: ribbon breaks into silver shards stretched along a cinematic tunnel, clear depth and vanishing point, sparse vermilion light. Panel 4: silver particles gather back into the same folded ribbon, quiet charcoal atmosphere. High-end experimental digital sculpture, believable physically based material, crisp silhouette, sophisticated restrained composition, charcoal and silver with vermilion as the only accent, no purple, no neon haze, no generic spheres, no copied artwork. Four distinct shot scales, continuity of shape. This is a static concept storyboard, not a functioning website.
```
